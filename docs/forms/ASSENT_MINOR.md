## What is this?

I am making a web app that watches blinks through a webcam. I want to find out whether blinks and
how long eyes stay closed change when people feel sleepy. The app is not a medical test and will
not tell you anything about your health.

## What would I do?

- Come to 2 or more sessions of about 45 minutes, one in the morning and one in the evening.
- Sit at a laptop with a webcam and do your own homework or computer work for about 25 minutes.
- Every 5 minutes, tap a number from 1 to 9 to say how sleepy you feel.
- Twice per session, press a key as fast as you can when a counter appears (3 minutes each).
- You do **not** have to stay up late or change your sleep.

## What is saved?

- The webcam picture never leaves the laptop and is never saved. No videos or photos.
- Only numbers are saved, under a code instead of your name.

## Do I have to?

No. It is up to you. You can stop at any time, even in the middle of a session, and nobody will be
upset. You can also ask for your numbers to be deleted.

If you feel very sleepy afterwards, do not drive, cycle, or do anything risky.
