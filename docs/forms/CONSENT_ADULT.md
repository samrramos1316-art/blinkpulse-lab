**Study:** Does eyelid behaviour on a webcam change with how sleepy people feel?
**Researcher:** [name], [school], student project. **Sponsor:** [teacher/mentor name, contact].

## What the study is about

I am testing whether an experimental web app, BlinkPulse Lab, can follow changes in eyelid
behaviour (blinks and how long the eyes are closed) and whether those changes go along with how
sleepy people say they feel. The app is a student project. It is not a medical device and does
not diagnose anything.

## What you would do

- Take part in at least 2 sessions of about 45 minutes, on different days: one in the morning
  (before 10:00) and one in the evening (19:00 to 21:30), at times that suit you.
- In each session: sit in front of a laptop with a webcam, rate your sleepiness on a 1-9 scale
  every 5 minutes with one tap, do two short 3-minute reaction-time tasks (press a key when a
  counter appears), and do about 25 minutes of your own ordinary computer work.
- You will not be asked to change your sleep, stay awake, or change what you eat or drink.

## What is recorded

- The webcam image is processed **only on the laptop** and is **never saved or sent anywhere**.
  No video, photos, or face shapes are kept.
- Only numbers are saved: blink timings and eye-closure measures, your sleepiness ratings, your
  reaction times, and whether you wore glasses or contacts.
- Your data is labelled with a code (for example P03), not your name. The list linking codes to
  names is kept on paper, separately, and destroyed when the study ends.
- Results are published only as group summaries and per-code numbers. Nothing that identifies
  you is published.

## Risks and benefits

- Risks are minimal: about the same as doing ordinary computer work. You may find the tasks a bit
  boring.
- There is no direct benefit to you. You help test whether this kind of measurement works.

## Your rights

- Taking part is voluntary. You can stop any session at any time, or leave the study, without
  giving a reason.
- You can ask for your data to be deleted at any time until [date when results are written up].
- Questions: [researcher contact]. Concerns about the study: [sponsor contact].

## Safety

If you feel very sleepy after a session, do not drive or do anything that needs you to be alert.
