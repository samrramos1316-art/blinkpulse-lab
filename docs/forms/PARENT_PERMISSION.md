**Study:** Does eyelid behaviour on a webcam change with how sleepy people feel?
**Researcher:** [name], [school], student project. **Sponsor:** [teacher/mentor name, contact].

## What the study is about

I am testing whether an experimental web app, BlinkPulse Lab, can follow changes in eyelid
behaviour (blinks and how long the eyes are closed) and whether those changes go along with how
sleepy people say they feel. The app is a student project. It is not a medical device and does
not diagnose anything.

## What your child would do

- Take part in at least 2 sessions of about 45 minutes, on different days: one in the morning
  (before 10:00) and one in the evening (19:00 to 21:30, never later), at times that suit your
  family.
- In each session: sit in front of a laptop with a webcam, rate their sleepiness on a 1-9 scale
  every 5 minutes with one tap, do two short 3-minute reaction-time tasks, and do about 25 minutes
  of their own ordinary schoolwork or computer work.
- Your child will **not** be asked to change their sleep, stay awake, or change what they eat or
  drink.

## What is recorded

- The webcam image is processed **only on the laptop** and is **never saved or sent anywhere**.
  No video, photos, or face shapes are kept.
- Only numbers are saved: blink timings and eye-closure measures, sleepiness ratings, reaction
  times, and whether glasses or contacts were worn.
- Data is labelled with a code (for example P03), not a name. The list linking codes to names is
  kept on paper, separately, and destroyed when the study ends.
- Results are published only as group summaries and per-code numbers. Nothing that identifies your
  child is published.

## Risks and benefits

- Risks are minimal: about the same as doing ordinary schoolwork on a computer.
- There is no direct benefit to your child.

## Your and your child's rights

- Taking part is voluntary. Your child can stop any session at any time, or leave the study,
  without giving a reason, and so can you on their behalf.
- You can ask for the data to be deleted at any time until [date when results are written up].
- Questions: [researcher contact]. Concerns about the study: [sponsor contact].

## Safety

If your child feels very sleepy after a session, they should not drive, cycle, or do anything that
needs them to be alert.
